HEXCORE CITY — DESTINATION INTERIOR & ENTERTAINMENT PASS

Each public destination now receives a themed scene on its first visit.
Cyber_Arena: luminous arena rings; Where Light Dies: bioluminescent interior forest; Untamed Horizon: motorsport orbital track; Forgotten Ashes: gothic spires; Hardware: engineering displays; Arcade: game cabinets; Museum: world exhibits.
Other rooms include a shared art installation.

To reduce unnecessary rendering cost, galleries are constructed only when a visitor enters. Animation also runs only for the occupied room. Current coordinates, menus, NPCs, main exterior, and welcome loading messages are unchanged.

The scene is procedural artwork rather than photorealistic 3D assets. Full browser / device performance remains to be tested.
