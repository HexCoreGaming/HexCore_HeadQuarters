CHANGED:

- Building interiors remain separate instanced rooms for performance; exiting an interior returns to its actual exterior building entrance.
- HexCore Tower stays a corporate building; original elevator, corporate departments, HQ, Admin dialogue, games and city remain unchanged.

HOW TO EXPLORE:
1. Exit the original Headquarters to HexCore City.
2. Cross the existing eastern bridge to the HexCore Tower island (approximately X 224 / Z 110).
3. Continue EAST across the illuminated boulevard to the public district (X ~287 onward, Z ~110).
4. Walk to the door side of a public building and press E / mobile Interact to enter. Walk inside to its info representative; exit back to its doorway.
5. The district directory shows information; it does not teleport visitors from the tower.
