HEXCORE CITY // ZIP 04 — NPC SPAWN & FINAL WEBSITE PASS

This is a FULL cumulative project, based on ZIP 03. Copy all contents to your GitHub Desktop repository; merge folders; do not delete other files.

FIXED:
- Headquarters NPCs are constructed BEFORE createPlayer runs. Reception representative and other headquarters NPCs exist on first player frame.
- City pedestrian/social NPC system is also constructed BEFORE createPlayer runs, rather than being created in the final delayed world job.
- NPC update loops run from the first animation frame, independently of city construction.
- The five-second delay now applies only to the outside environment and destination systems, not to the NPCs.
- The progressive world InstancedMesh batches now compute bounding spheres and permit view-frustum culling; previously all offscreen batches were rendered.
- Remaining environment stages retain nearby-to-farther construction.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:
- Corporate department NPCs and their interiors are part of the progressively initialized destination systems. They are NOT all loaded before the player; doing so would reintroduce the startup freezes. Only the initial headquarters and citywide visitor population are pre-spawned.
- This release DOES NOT deliver finished cinematic AAA assets or a complete production backend.
- These changes have been statically checked, not performance-tested in a browser.
