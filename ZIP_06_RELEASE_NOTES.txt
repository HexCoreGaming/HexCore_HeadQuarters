HEXCORE // ZIP 06 // CENTRAL WORLD LAYOUT REBUILD

WORLD PLAN:
- HexCore Tower is the actual world center at (0,300).
- Original headquarters/player start remains near (0,0), with a long ceremonial approach.
- Game showcases, museum, arcade, hardware, merchandise and future destinations are positioned around the central tower, not behind it.
- Public-building entrances share coordinates with the visible structures, using world/worldLayout.js.
