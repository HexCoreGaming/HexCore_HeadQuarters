HEXCORE CITY // ARCHITECTURAL ASSET PASS

Complete cumulative project based on the Bioluminescent Sanctuaries ZIP.

NEW: custom lofted curved facade meshes instead of more box primitives; broad front apertures aligned to existing interaction anchors; curved glass-like canopy shells; family-specific elven buttress silhouettes for each of the 16 destinations; sculpted ribs and edge-light traces for HexCore Tower. Assets are procedurally authored as proper indexed BufferGeometry in Three.js, not externally modeled GLBs. Generated progressively within the existing welcome loading sequence.

No physical building positions, doors, or NPC content intentionally changed. Existing old structural masses are still present beneath the new shells and may need removal during later art integration. Rendering/performance has not been browser-tested.
